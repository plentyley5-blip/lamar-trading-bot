Frequently asked questions

Does LM Analyzer execute trades automatically?

Manual LM Analyzer chart analysis returns analysis results; it does not itself prove a broker trade was executed. LM AUTO TRADER is a separate MT5-linked feature with its own backend and execution controls.

Which charts should I upload?

The core chart-analysis flow requires both a 4H chart and a 15M chart. Optional timeframes can be selected where supported. Keep current price digits and the price scale readable.

Can free users access High Impact News?

No. High Impact News events, related-instrument outlooks and AI news signals are available only to the owner or users with an active paid subscription.

Does LM Analyzer guarantee profitable trades?

No. AI analysis is informational, can be wrong or incomplete and is not a guarantee of future market movement. Users remain responsible for risk decisions and broker execution.
